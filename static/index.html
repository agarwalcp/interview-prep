Before you begin

  • Answer as you would in a real interview: explain the idea, give reasoning and an example.
  • Technical answers are scored on correctness, relevance, completeness and explanation. Repeating the question or listing keywords scores low.
  • HR answers should describe one real situation, what you did, and the result.
  • Scores and expected concepts are shown only on the results screen.
  • Spoken answers also get a delivery estimate (filler words, pace, repetition). It is not a measure of your confidence or emotions.
